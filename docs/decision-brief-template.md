# Decision brief template

Use at the end of a GRILL session. Keep it short enough to read before implementation.

## Goal

What outcome are we trying to create?

## Users and jobs

Who uses it, in what situation, and what job are they trying to finish?

## Success criteria

How will we know this worked? Prefer observable outcomes over opinions.

## Scope

What is included in the first slice?

## Non-goals

What are we deliberately not doing now?

## Constraints

Time, budget, platform, regulatory, team, compatibility, or operational limits.
